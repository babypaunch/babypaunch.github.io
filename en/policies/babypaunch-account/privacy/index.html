Privacy · BabyPaunch Account

BabyPaunch Account Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BabyPaunch handles information when providing Google sign-in and a shared account for its apps.

First effective: October 11, 2026
Last revised: October 11, 2026

BabyPaunch Account is the shared sign-in service for apps operated by BabyPaunch. It is currently used for online puzzle requests in Art Of Jigsaw. When you sign in with Google, the account server at auth.babypaunch.com handles authentication.

Information processed and why

We use the account identifier, email address, and profile information (name and photo, when provided) returned during Google sign-in to identify your account and sign you in. The account server stores a user record and the information needed to link the sign-in, then issues a user authentication token to the app. BabyPaunch servers do not receive or store your Google password.

Art Of Jigsaw sends the authentication token, puzzle column and row counts, and image dimensions to gate.babypaunch.com. The server checks the token with the account server and returns puzzle piece paths. The image file used for the puzzle and your completion records are not included in this request.

Information kept on your device and our servers

Art Of Jigsaw keeps its bundled images, images you add, downloaded artwork, puzzle records, and scores on your device. Images you add are not uploaded to the BabyPaunch account or puzzle servers. The authentication token is used while the game runs; the app removes the temporary file used by its external request worker after reading it.

For operations and abuse prevention, server and proxy access logs may contain an IP address, request path, time, and error details. These records are used for service operation and security checks.

Other services

Google handles sign-in, subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Searching for and downloading public artwork in Art Of Jigsaw may use The Metropolitan Museum of Art services. This account service does not process advertising or payments.

Retention and deletion

We keep account information to provide the account service. To request account deletion or access or correction of your information, contact us below. After verifying your identity, we will handle the information associated with the account and explain the deletion scope if it is connected to another BabyPaunch service. You can remove local images through the game's deletion feature. You can remove all stored game information, including local records, by clearing the app's stored data on your device. Server access logs may be kept for the period needed for operations and security.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy to reflect changes to connected apps, sign-in, or data processing.
