Privacy · BabyPaunch

Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be handled when you use the BabyPaunch website.

First effective: August 26, 2026
Last revised: August 28, 2026

Information we collect

BabyPaunch does not provide accounts, sign-in, comments, or contact forms on this website. We do not directly collect or separately store visitors' personal information.

Website hosting

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process access logs or device information to operate and secure its service. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Embedded content and external links

Music and blog pages may include content from YouTube and Spotify. When you play or use that content, those services may process cookies, IP addresses, device information, and usage activity under the Google Privacy Policy and Spotify Privacy Policy.

When you follow a link to another website, that website's privacy policy applies to its handling of information.

Visitor analytics and consent

BabyPaunch loads Google Analytics only after a visitor allows analytics. Google Analytics processes visited pages, traffic sources, scrolling and outbound-link clicks, and approximate location, device, and browser information so we can understand which posts and channels are useful. Google uses IP addresses to derive approximate location and protect its service but does not provide raw IP addresses in our reports.

Google Signals and user-provided data collection are disabled. BabyPaunch does not send names, email addresses, or its own User-IDs to Google Analytics. Google Search Console is connected only to review search visibility and site status; it does not install a separate tracking script in a visitor's browser.

International processing by Google

If analytics is allowed, the information above may be sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection at the time of a visit to analytics servers operated by Google LLC and its affiliates. Google processes information to provide and secure the service and produce statistics in countries where its facilities are located, including the United States. The retention periods follow the Google Analytics settings in the table above. Visitors who do not want this international processing can decline analytics or withdraw consent through the setting below.

Declining, withdrawing consent, and deletion

If you decline, the Google Analytics script is not loaded and every site feature remains available. You can change your choice at any time through at the bottom of the page. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; cookies already stored can be removed through your browser's site-data controls. Requests concerning deletion of existing analytics data can be sent to the email address below.

See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on for Google's processing and additional controls.

Advertising

BabyPaunch does not currently use Google AdSense advertising code. Before advertising is introduced, this policy and the consent interface will be updated to cover advertising cookies, personalized advertising, and any additional consent choices that are required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, its services, or applicable requirements change. We do not update dates on a fixed schedule; when the policy content changes, we record the last revised date and the change below.
