Accessibility · BabyPaunch

Accessibility

BabyPaunch aims to make its content readable and navigable by more people.

First published: August 26, 2026
Last updated: August 26, 2026

Our accessibility goal

We design and improve this website with the goal of meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA. New pages and features follow the same standard.

Supported features

  • A skip link that moves directly to the main content
  • Navigation and tag filters that work with a keyboard
  • Visible indicators for the current location and keyboard focus
  • Headings, landmarks, link names, and image descriptions for screen readers
  • Language information for Korean and English pages and passages
  • Support for reduced-motion and high-contrast browser preferences
  • Page zoom and responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens

Known limitations

YouTube and Spotify players are provided by third parties. Accessibility and keyboard behavior inside those players may vary according to each service's implementation.
