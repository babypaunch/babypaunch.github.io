Privacy · Netflix Mania

Netflix Mania Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Netflix Mania handles data and limits its access to the Netflix website.

First effective: September 2, 2026
Last revised: September 2, 2026

Netflix Mania is a Chrome extension for controlling the Netflix web player's built-in playback-speed menu with the keyboard.

Information collected or stored

Netflix Mania does not collect or store personal information, settings, or usage records.

Information not collected

Netflix Mania does not collect, sell, or transmit personal identifiers, Netflix sign-in information, payment information, browsing history, video viewing history, or page content.

Website access

The extension runs only on https://www.netflix.com/* to detect keyboard shortcuts and select Netflix's built-in playback-speed menu. It does not run on other websites or communicate with a developer-operated server.

External support link

When a user deliberately selects the support button, the Ko-fi page at https://ko-fi.com/babypaunch opens. The extension does not directly collect or process payment information. The privacy policies of Ko-fi and its payment services apply. Support is optional, and every extension feature remains free.

Unofficial extension

This extension is not created, approved, endorsed by, or affiliated with Netflix.

Revision history

We do not update dates on a fixed schedule; when the policy content changes, we record the last revised date and the change below.
