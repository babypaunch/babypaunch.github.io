Privacy · Laftel Mania

Laftel Mania Privacy Policy

This policy explains the setting Laftel Mania stores and why it requests access to websites.

First effective: August 25, 2026
Last revised: August 25, 2026

Laftel Mania stores one playback-speed value in Chrome sync storage so it can remember and reapply the speed selected for Laftel videos.

Information stored

  • The video playback speed most recently selected by the user

This information remains in the user's Chrome sync storage. The developer does not transmit it to or collect it on a separate server.

Information not collected

Laftel Mania does not collect or sell personal identifiers, sign-in information, payment information, browsing history, video viewing history, or page content.

Website access

Because Laftel videos may play inside an external HTTPS player frame, the extension requests permission to run on HTTPS pages. Its script runs only when the current frame or a parent frame belongs to laftel.net and exits immediately on other pages.

External support link

When a user deliberately selects the support button, the Ko-fi page at https://ko-fi.com/babypaunch opens. The extension does not directly collect or process payment information. The privacy policies of Ko-fi and PayPal apply. Support is optional, and every extension feature remains free.

Unofficial extension

This extension is not created, approved, endorsed by, or affiliated with Laftel.

Revision history

We do not update dates on a fixed schedule; when the policy content changes, we record the last revised date and the change below.
