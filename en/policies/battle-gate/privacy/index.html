Privacy · Battle Gate

Battle Gate Privacy Policy

Battle Gate is a game access service operated by BabyPaunch. The sections below distinguish shared platform processing from additional rules for games currently offered. A new game will receive its own disclosure; the AOJ section does not automatically apply to other games.

First effective: October 18, 2026
Last revised: October 21, 2026

1. Shared Battle Gate platform

Sign-in and accounts

We use the account identifier, name, and email address returned by Google sign-in to identify your account and sign you in. We do not receive or store your Google password. Sessions are stored as hashes of random tokens with expiration times and expire seven days after issue or renewal.

Access and external services

Game admission permissions and access records are managed per game. Google authentication is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The web and Android apps use the same production service. Battle Gate currently has no advertising, payment, or user-to-user chat features.

Retention and security

Account information is kept while you use the service. We use HTTPS and HttpOnly and Secure login cookies. Operational and security server or proxy logs may include IP addresses, request paths, times, and errors. Backups and access logs may be kept for the period needed for operation and security.

2. Game-specific rules — Art Of Jigsaw co-op

Rooms and shared progress

We process room titles, participant account identifiers and names, connection times, puzzle settings, and shared board progress. Members of the same room can see its title, participant names, and progress. An invite code can be shared with others; a signed-in user with that code must still meet room capacity and other admission conditions.

Selected shared photos

Only a photo explicitly selected by the host and confirmed for sharing is converted to PNG in the browser and uploaded to Battle Gate. The server validates it, removes metadata, and serves it to admitted members of that room. Other device photos not shared in the room are not uploaded.

Artwork and piece generation

Public artwork searches may send search terms and request information to The Metropolitan Museum of Art. Artwork image requests may pass through BabyPaunch Access Gate. Logic Hub receives grid dimensions, image dimensions, and generation values to create piece paths, but not shared photo files or Google account information.

Room retention and scope

After nobody remains connected for about five minutes, the room and shared photo are removed. Failed photo deletions are recorded and retried. This section covers only AOJ co-op in Battle Gate. For the separate web or local single-player edition, see the Art Of Jigsaw Single-player Privacy Policy.

3. Account and data deletion

Delete in the app or website

After signing in, use Delete account on the account page to remove your account, sessions, admission records, participation records, owned co-op rooms, and shared photos from service data. Puzzle progress in rooms owned by someone else remains, while your participation records are removed.

Request deletion without the app

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, request deletion at babypaunch@gmail.com. After verifying your identity, we will explain the scope and result. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, or original photos. Backups or logs needed for security or legal reasons may be excluded; we will explain any such scope on request.

Content safety, reporting and blocking data

We use policy versions and acceptance times, block relationships, and report parties, rooms, reasons, details, and status for safety and operator review. Reports are periodically cleared using a 30-day threshold, and account deletion removes related acceptances, blocks, and reports. A hashed account identifier and restriction expiry can remain after deletion to enforce a restriction for up to 30 days from issue, with periodic cleanup. We do not retain report-specific photo copies. See the Community Policy, Reports and Blocking for procedures and prohibited content.

4. New games and policy changes

Per-game management

Each new game will have a separate section identifying its name, mode, processed information, recipients, transfers, retention, and deletion. This does not authorize undisclosed collection or sharing for games not yet offered. We update the policy and revision history when processing changes.
